## 建議命名規則

```text
logo-{language}-{color}.{ext}
logo-{language-combo}-{color}.{ext}
auxiliary-{type}-{color}.{ext}
```

範例：

- `logo-eng-black.png`
- `logo-ch-en-black.png`
- `auxiliary-mark-orange.png`
